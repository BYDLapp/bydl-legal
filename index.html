Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 July 2026

This explains what BYDL collects, why, and what you can do about it. We are the data controller. Contact: privacy@bydl.app.

1. What we collect

BYDL has no user accounts, sign-in or profile — we do not collect your email address, phone number or any Apple/Google account identifier.

  • What you tell us: first name, age, what you do, rough monthly income, what you can put aside, the country and lifestyle you pick, extra things you want, and anything you write in your daily log or spending list. This is sent to our server to generate your estimate and is not stored afterwards — see section 7.
  • Purchase data: which plan you bought and when. Card details go to Apple or Google — we never see or store them.
  • Technical data: device type, OS version, app version and language, as made available by Apple/Google's standard platform APIs.

2. Financial information

Income, savings and spending figures are financial data. We keep them encrypted, never sell them, and never share them with advertisers. You can skip the income question and still use the app.

3. Why we use it

  • To build your cost estimate, monthly target and plan — the service you asked for (contract).
  • To run your spending log and saving streak, kept on your device (contract).
  • To meet tax, accounting and legal duties (legal obligation).

4. Artificial intelligence

To create your estimate and plan we send your answers as text to an AI provider (Anthropic). We do not send your name, email or account identifier. Under our agreement the provider processes it to return a result and does not use it to train models.

5. Who else sees it

  • Hosting: our cloud provider (Render), in the United States (Ohio).
  • AI processing: Anthropic (United States).
  • Payments: Apple and Google.

We do not sell or share personal data for advertising. BYDL contains no third-party ad SDKs and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Where data leaves the EEA or the UK — for example to our US-based hosting or AI provider — we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and send only what the service needs.

7. How long we keep it

We do not have user accounts, so there is no account data to retain. Your plan answers are processed to generate your estimate and are not stored afterwards — nothing persists on our server past that single request. Invoice records from purchases, once made, are kept for 10 years where tax law requires.

Children's Privacy

Last updated 31 July 2026

BYDL is open to people of any age, but the rules change for younger users. This notice explains how.

If you are under 13

Before you continue past the age question, the app asks you to hand the phone to a parent or guardian and asks them to confirm.

For users under 13 we do the following, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 of the GDPR:

  • We collect the minimum needed to run the plan: first name, age, country and lifestyle choices.
  • We do not ask for an email address, phone number, precise location or photograph.
  • We do not show subscription offers, and no purchase can be made.
  • We never use a child's data for advertising, profiling or behavioural targeting.

A parent or guardian may email privacy@bydl.app at any time with questions or to withdraw consent. As explained in the Privacy Policy, BYDL has no accounts and does not store plan answers on our server, so there is no stored record to delete on request — a plan simply stops being generated if consent is withdrawn.

If you are 13 to 15

In countries where the digital age of consent is 16 (much of the EEA), a parent or guardian must confirm before we process your data. Purchases are not available.

If you are 16 or 17

You can use all features except buying a subscription, which requires an adult.

Verification

The in-app check is a simple adult-presence question. It is not proof of identity. Parents remain responsible for supervising their child's use of the app and their device's purchase settings.

Reporting

If you believe a child has used BYDL in a way that concerns you, email privacy@bydl.app with what you observed and we will look into it.
